# Pilot Guide Outline

Role:

- follow-up booklet after first interest
- practical and calmer than `First Contact`

Audience:

- shop owner who wants to understand the first test in concrete terms

Format:

- Danish
- A5 booklet
- 8 pages
- imposed as 4 A4 booklet pages

Page jobs:

1. Cover: small first pilot
2. What we need from the shop
3. What is included in the first setup
4. What it can run on
5. Customer flow
6. Shop flow
7. Errors, stop-paths, and first week
8. Back cover with next step and contact
