# P4P Brussels 2026 One-Pagers

This hackathon packet is now intentionally split into two versions.

It should not try to do the whitepaper job.

That comes later.

## Active versions

- `one-pager-brussels-2026-nontechnical.md`
  Short group-facing version for teammates, judges, and first conversation.
- `one-pager-brussels-2026-technical.md`
  Denser group-facing version for developers and technical reviewers who want the system
  boundary, protocol loop, and anti-capture logic.

## Print files

- `one-pager-brussels-2026-nontechnical.pdf`
- `one-pager-brussels-2026-technical.pdf`

## Companion Working Note

- `hackathon-checklist-brussels-2026.md`
  Team checklist for what can be built during the hackathon, what should not be
  claimed, and what kind of EU or public-interest support would actually help
  the team continue afterwards.
- `hackathon-checklist-brussels-2026.html`
- `hackathon-checklist-brussels-2026.pdf`

## Rule

Use the non-technical version first unless the room is already asking:

- how is this different from a normal app?
- where does the registry sit?
- what exactly is the protocol claim?
- why is this not just another ordering backend?

Then switch to the technical version as the team's deeper explanation layer.

The whitepaper still needs its own proper write-up later.
