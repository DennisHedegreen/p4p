DIRECT PICKUP ORDERS
WITHOUT MARKETPLACE CAPTURE.

Use this when the group needs the shortest version first: the customer finds the shop, sees the menu, places a pickup order, and the shop receives it directly.

The core claim is simple: the customer should be able to reach the shop directly without a marketplace owning first contact.

The Problem

Food platforms look convenient, but they become permanent middlemen. The shop pays fees, loses visibility, and the customer remembers the app instead of the place that makes the food.

Who It Is For

  • small pizza, kebab, grill, burger, and takeaway shops
  • owner-run places that need a simple direct channel
  • nearby customers who just need menu, order, and pickup

What The Group Can Show

  • one shop page with menu and opening state
  • QR code or direct link to the shop page
  • simple pickup-order flow
  • small operator screen for incoming orders
  • received and ready order status
  • payment at pickup first
QR/link -> menu -> order -> shop screen -> received -> ready -> pickup

Why This Is Strong In A Hackathon

  • concrete user and visible pain
  • easy to explain in one minute
  • visual live demo instead of abstract slides
  • small enough to scope honestly
  • deeper protocol story exists if the team wants ambition

How A 5-Person Team Can Split It

  1. customer flow and menu page
  2. operator screen and status UI
  3. backend and order transport
  4. design and pitch
  5. integration and live demo

Hard Scope Boundary

In the hackathon we do not build delivery logistics, online payment processing, customer accounts, loyalty systems, full federation, or a production-security story.

Not: "Uber Eats but open source."

Honest Claim

This is a direct pickup-ordering proof for local shops. If it works, it shows that a small shop can have a direct digital route back to the customer without handing the relationship to a marketplace.

What The Team Already Has

  • Owner-facing concept: pizza4people.com
  • Repo and protocol layer: github.com/DennisHedegreen/p4p
  • Boundary: use what already exists, but keep the demo smaller than the full repo story
